Three questions.
Three outfits.

Peppi asks only what changes the answer, then does the styling: the right formality, layers for the weather, colors that work together, and a top, bottom and shoes in every outfit.

  1. i.

    Choose your occasion

    32 occasions, from a job interview to a wedding, an airport day or a masquerade ball.

  2. ii.

    Pick the day and time

    Today or tomorrow, morning to night. Peppi gets the weather where you'll be, or the forecast when it's later.

  3. iii.

    Set the formality

    A slider from 1 to 10, because "work" can mean jeans or a client dinner.

  4. iv.

    Swipe through three outfits

    Three different approaches, such as Classic, Bold and Relaxed, each with a real photo and why it works.

Want more control? Open Refine further to add notes ("lots of walking"), pick a mood, choose one of 10 style inspirations, or set color rules like "no black". Peppi follows them in the photo too.

The details

Small things that make the outfits yours.

{{icon:person}}

Photos of people like you

Every outfit has a real photo, chosen to match your gender and age, showing the whole look from head to toe. The description is written from the photo, so the words and the picture agree.

{{icon:sun}}

Weather where you'll be

Peppi checks the weather where you are, or where you'll be, for the time you picked. Choose "Today, Evening" in the morning and you get the evening forecast, not the morning temperature.

{{icon:palette}}

Your color rules

Say "no black" or "something in green", and Peppi applies it to the outfit and to the photo it picks.

Colors: no black, earthy tones
{{icon:sparkle}}

Ten style inspirations

Build all three outfits around the style of a fashion icon, from "Creative / Retro / Bold" to quiet tailoring. Shown with photos of everyday people in that style.

{{icon:heart}}

Save the looks you love

Tap the heart on a photo to keep a look. Each saved look shows when it will be removed, and signed-in looks are kept for 43 days.

{{icon:repeat}}

Fair counting

An outfit request is only counted when all three outfits arrive. Asking again with nothing changed is free, and you won't see the same photo twice in a month.

Peppi's Style DNA

Learns your taste from every look you save.

After 10 saved looks, Peppi works out your style directions and the colors and fits you come back to, and uses them in every outfit request.
