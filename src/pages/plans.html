How plans work

Clear rules, no surprises.

Your month starts on your day
Outfits and try-ons start over every month from the day your plan started, not on the 1st.
Monthly or yearly
A yearly plan is paid once for 12 months. Your outfits and try-ons still start over every month.
Automatic payment, your choice
On by default, so your plan renews. Turn it off and the plan simply ends after the month or year you paid for.
Upgrade at once
Moving from Signature to Couture starts straight away, with a fresh month.
Downgrade or cancel at the end of the period
You keep everything until then. Peppi shows what you'll lose before you confirm.
7-outfit packs
Out of outfits? Add 7 more, to use within 7 days. They're used after your plan's outfits.
Virtual try-on is 18+
Try-on and measurements use full-body photos, so they're only for people aged 18 and over. Younger Couture members get the outfits and Style DNA.
Leaving Couture
Your try-on photos, measurements and try-ons are deleted when Couture ends.
Paid through the store
Purchases go through Google Play or the App Store, and you manage or cancel them there too.

Limits and features may change as Peppi grows. The app always shows the current ones before you buy. See the terms of service for the full rules.
