What's in it

Six things your Style DNA tells you.

Each one is a part of your Style DNA page in the app. The sample card at the top shows the first two: the bars are your style directions, the chips under them your preferences.

1. Your style directions

Which styles you lean towards, as a percentage for each of 10: Classic, Smart Casual, Quiet Luxury, Minimalist, Sport Elegant, Streetwear, Romantic, Bohemian, Edgy and Bold. For example, Classic 82%. A look can be both Classic and Quiet Luxury, so they don't add up to 100.

2. Your preferences

The details you pick again and again: colors, fit, patterns and layering. Each is marked High, Medium or Low, for example "High: Navy" or "Medium: Layering".

3. How you dress for each occasion

Where your taste shifts with the plan for the day, for example "Job interview: more formal, leans Classic" or "Weekend with friends: more relaxed".

4. What Peppi learned about you

A one-line summary of your style and a few observations in plain words, like "Prefers one statement piece with quiet basics". If one isn't you, tap Not me and Peppi leaves it out.

5. How sure Peppi is

The more looks you save, the surer it gets: Low under 15 saved looks, Medium from 15, High from 30. The app tells you how many more looks reach the next level.

6. What changed

After each update, you see where your taste moved: "↑ was 28%" on a direction, a level that changed, a new preference, occasion or insight, and a graph of your taste over time.

Recent taste counts most

Your newest looks lead. Older ones still count.

Peppi groups your saved looks in tens, newest first. Each older group counts a little over half as much as the group after it, so your DNA follows how your taste is changing without forgetting where it started.

When it updates

It grows with every ten saves.

Your first DNA at ten saved looks
Save looks with the heart. The counter on Saved looks shows how many are left.
An update after every ten more
Peppi recalculates from all your looks and rewrites the summary, then tells you what changed.
Deleting a look takes it out at once
If a look doesn't feel like you, delete it. Your DNA is recalculated straight away without it.
Expired photos still count
A saved look's photo is removed after 43 days, but the look keeps counting towards your DNA until you delete it.
In every request

Two outfits that are you. One that stretches you.

With a Style DNA, the first two outfits follow it and the third goes a little further, so you still discover something new. What you ask for always comes first: the occasion, the weather, the formality, your color rules and any style inspiration you pick.

The photos follow your DNA too. Among suitable photos, the ones closest to your style are chosen first.
