What to wear to a graduation

For a graduation, smart casual is the safest choice: polished enough for a formal ceremony and photos, comfortable enough for a long, often warm midday event. Aim for clean, well-fitted pieces in solid or subtle colors, and let your shoes and fit do the work of making the outfit look considered.

Classic, bold or relaxed.

Tips

  • Check the dress code on the invitation or school website; if the ceremony is outdoors or in a gym, lean slightly more casual.
  • At around 21°C, a light layer such as a blazer or cardigan is enough, since it's easy to remove once you're in the sun.
  • Choose shoes you can stand in for hours, such as loafers, low block heels, or leather derbies, and break them in beforehand.
  • Stick to a palette of two to three colors, and avoid anything that clashes with a black or dark gown.
  • Pay attention to fit at the shoulders and hem, and keep accessories simple: one statement piece like a watch, earrings, or a tie is plenty.

What to avoid

  • Overly casual items like sneakers, graphic tees, or distressed denim.
  • Bright white or very light outfits that may show dust, sweat marks, or wrinkles from sitting in the sun.
  • Anything you haven't worn before, especially new shoes that might cause blisters on a long day.

Questions

What should I wear under a graduation gown?

Wear whatever you'd wear to the event itself, since the gown covers most of your outfit. Focus on the parts that show, like your neckline, sleeves, and shoes. Keep the layers underneath comfortable, because gowns can trap heat.

Is it okay to wear a dress to a graduation?

Yes, a dress is a great choice for a graduation. Pick a midi or maxi length in a fabric suited to the temperature, and add a light jacket or cardigan if you want more polish.

What should men wear to a graduation?

A well-fitted blazer or waistcoat with tailored trousers and a collared shirt works for most graduations. Open-collar or a tie can be dressed up or down depending on the event's formality.
