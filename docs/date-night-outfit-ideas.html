Date night outfit ideas

For a date night, smart casual is the safest target: dressed up enough to look like you made an effort, relaxed enough to feel like yourself. At around 19°C in the evening, a shirt or top with trousers or dark jeans and one layer you can take off works for almost anyone. Tips

  • Aim for one step above what you'd wear to a casual day out, and match the level of the place you're going: a bar and a bistro are different dress codes.
  • At about 19°C, bring one layer you can add or remove, like a jacket, overshirt or light coat. Evenings cool off faster than you expect once you're outside.
  • Choose shoes that are comfortable for walking and standing. Leather loafers, boots or clean minimal sneakers all work, depending on how dressy the night is.
  • Stick to two or three colors. One bold piece, like burgundy or mustard, looks deliberate; several competing colors can look busy.
  • Pay attention to fit at the shoulders and hem. A slightly tailored fit does more for a date look than any single statement piece.

What to avoid

  • Wearing anything you haven't worn before without trying it on first; comfort shows in how you move and sit.
  • Overdressing to a casual spot, which can make the evening feel more formal than you intended.
  • Strong fragrance and heavily branded clothing, which can be distracting in a close setting.

Questions

What should I wear on a first date?

Choose something smart casual that you feel confident in: well-fitted trousers or dark jeans with a shirt, knit or blouse, and comfortable shoes. Aim to look put together without looking like you're going to an interview.

How dressy should a date night outfit be?

One step above your everyday look is usually right. Match the venue: a casual restaurant suits clean jeans and a nice top or shirt, while a formal dinner calls for tailored pieces and dressier shoes.

What do I wear on a date when it's cold outside?

Layer. A shirt or fitted top under a jacket, coat or knit lets you stay warm outdoors and comfortable indoors. Choose a layer that looks good on its own when you take it off.
