Three questions.
Three outfits.

Peppi asks only what changes the answer, then does the styling: formality, layers for the weather, colors that work together.

  1. i.

    Choose your occasion

    32 occasions, from a job interview to a wedding, an airport day or a masquerade ball.

  2. ii.

    Pick the day and time

    Today or tomorrow, morning to night. Peppi gets the weather where you'll be.

  3. iii.

    Set the formality

    A slider from 1 to 10, and every number has a name: 1 Loungewear, 2 Very casual, 3 Casual, 4 Casual chic, 5 Smart casual, 6 Business casual, 7 Business, 8 Dressy, 9 Formal, 10 Black tie.

  4. iv.

    Swipe through three outfits

    Classic, Bold and Relaxed, each with a real photo and why it works.
