What to wear to dinner with friends

For dinner with friends, smart casual is the safe target: polished enough to feel like you made an effort, relaxed enough that nobody feels overdressed. Aim for well-fitting clothes, one clean layer, and shoes you'd be happy to sit in for two hours. The example below is for an evening at 8°C with a formality level of 5 out of 10.

Tips

  • Dress to the place, not the invitation: a casual bistro suits the Relaxed look, while a white-tablecloth spot calls for the Classic.
  • Layer for the temperature: at 8°C, a coat or jacket you can leave on the chair matters more than a heavy sweater.
  • Choose shoes you can walk and sit in comfortably; leather boots or clean sneakers beat anything you'll be adjusting all night.
  • Keep the palette to two or three colors so the outfit looks intentional. Neutrals like grey, black and camel are easy to combine.
  • Let one detail do the talking, such as a scarf, a tie, or a bold watch, and keep the rest simple.

What to avoid

  • Heavy athletic gear or worn-out sneakers, which can read as not trying at all.
  • Too many competing patterns or logos in one outfit.
  • Anything you have to keep fixing, like a scarf that slips or shoes that pinch.

Questions

What is smart casual for dinner with friends?

Smart casual means clean, well-fitting clothes that look intentional without being formal. A knit, tailored trousers or dark jeans, and a jacket or coat usually hit the mark. Skip suits unless the group has said it's a dressy occasion.

What should I wear to dinner with friends in cold weather?

Wear a base layer you're comfortable in, a sweater or knit, and an outer layer like a coat or denim jacket. Choose something easy to take off indoors, since restaurants can get warm once everyone arrives.

Is it okay to wear jeans to dinner with friends?

Yes, as long as they're dark, well-fitted and in good condition. Pair them with a blazer, a knit or a clean button-down so they don't look like everyday wear.
