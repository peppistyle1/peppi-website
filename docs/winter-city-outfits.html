Winter outfits for a day in the city

For a winter day in the city, aim for casual chic: warm layers, clean lines and one considered detail. A structured coat over a knit or turtleneck, with comfortable boots and well-fitted trousers, works for walking, cafes and an afternoon out, even at around freezing.

Tips

  • Dress for the temperature at its coldest hour, and plan for a warmer indoor space by keeping a layer you can remove.
  • Layer in thin pieces, like a knit under a coat, rather than one bulky item, so you can adjust as you move between cold streets and heated rooms.
  • Choose shoes you can walk in all day. Boots with a bit of grip are the safest choice for cold, possibly wet pavement.
  • Keep the palette tight: one or two strong colors with neutrals, such as cream, black or denim, so the outfit looks deliberate.
  • Add one detail that adds warmth or interest, like a beret, a scarf, gloves or a bag in a contrast color.

What to avoid

  • Thin fabrics that look nice but do not block wind, such as unlined linen or a light jacket with no insulation.
  • Shoes with smooth soles or open toes, which get slippery and cold quickly in winter.
  • Too many competing patterns or colors, which can make a cold-weather layered look feel busy.

Questions

What should I wear for a winter day in the city?

Wear a warm, structured coat over a knit or turtleneck, with comfortable trousers or a dress and boots with grip. Layer so you can adjust indoors and outdoors.

How do I stay warm in the city in winter without looking bulky?

Use thin, warm layers like a fine-knit turtleneck under a well-fitted coat. Choose a coat with some structure so it looks tailored rather than puffy, and add a scarf or hat for extra warmth at the neck and head.

What shoes are best for walking around the city in winter?

Boots with a sturdy, grippy sole and a comfortable fit are the best choice. Knee-high boots work well with trousers or a dress, but make sure you can walk comfortably in them for hours.
