What to wear to a wedding as a guest

For an evening wedding, aim for dressy but not formal: a polished outfit in a fabric that suits the season, with nothing that competes with the couple. When in doubt, choose something that looks finished from head to toe and avoid white, ivory and anything that looks like bridal wear. The example here is an evening wedding at 13°C, which calls for a substantial fabric or a layer you can keep on.

Classic, bold or relaxed.

Tips

  • Check the invitation for dress code cues like 'black tie optional' or 'cocktail,' and if the couple has a seasonal theme, follow it lightly.
  • At 13°C, bring a layer such as a wrap, blazer or light coat, since outdoor photos and evening air can feel cold even when the day was mild.
  • Choose closed-toe or dressy shoes you can stand in for hours; if the venue is a lawn or gravel, pick a block heel or flat dressy shoe.
  • Keep the palette to two or three main colors so bold pieces look intentional; deep jewel tones, navy, cream and metallics all work well.
  • Finish with simple details like a clutch, a watch or one statement earring, and keep hair and makeup polished but not overly formal.

What to avoid

  • White, ivory or cream full looks that could be mistaken for the bride's outfit.
  • Casual items such as denim, sneakers, graphic tees or flip-flops, even if the venue feels relaxed.
  • Anything so revealing or so sheer that it needs constant adjusting during the ceremony and dinner.

Questions

What should a man wear to an evening wedding as a guest?

A suit in navy or charcoal with a dress shirt and tie is the safest choice for a dressy evening wedding. A cream or light grey suit can work for a summer or daytime event, and a dark overcoat or topcoat covers the cool evening air well.

Is it okay to wear black to a wedding?

Black is acceptable at most evening weddings, especially when dressed up with color or texture in accessories. Some couples ask guests to avoid black for daytime or casual events, so check the invitation or wedding website if you're unsure.

How do I dress for an outdoor wedding without ruining my outfit?

Choose block heels or flat dressy shoes for grass and gravel, and bring a light layer for temperature changes. Fabrics that resist wrinkling and avoid trailing hems will keep you comfortable and presentable through the day.
