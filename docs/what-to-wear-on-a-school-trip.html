What to wear on a school trip

For a school trip, dress for comfort first: you'll likely be walking, sitting on a bus, and possibly standing around outdoors. Choose relaxed, easy-to-move clothes with a layer you can add or remove, and shoes you can walk in all day. Peppi builds outfits around the weather at your destination and how casual you want to be.

Tips

  • Check the forecast for the destination, not just your home town, since temperatures can differ a lot, especially for a morning start.
  • Wear layers: a tee under a light jacket, hoodie, or overshirt lets you adjust on a cool bus or a warm afternoon.
  • Break in your shoes before the trip. Sneakers or skate shoes with good support matter more than any other piece.
  • Choose jeans or pants that move well. Straight or relaxed cuts are comfortable for sitting and walking; very tight fits can feel restrictive after hours on your feet.
  • Keep colors simple with one brighter piece, such as a green or teal top, and neutrals like white, black, or denim for everything else. Pieces that mix easily make packing simpler.

What to avoid

  • Brand-new shoes you haven't walked in, since blisters can ruin a day out.
  • Heavy bags or big purses that dig into your shoulder; a small backpack distributes weight better.
  • Overly dressy or delicate clothes, like satin or white fabrics that show dirt, when you'll be on buses, grass, or crowded spaces.

Questions

What should I wear on a school trip in cold weather?

Layer a warm base tee or long-sleeve top under a fleece, hoodie, or light jacket. Jeans or sturdy pants and closed-toe sneakers keep you comfortable. Bring a layer you can take off indoors or when it warms up.

Are jeans okay for a school trip?

Yes, jeans are a practical choice for most school trips. Pick a comfortable, straight or relaxed cut that you can walk and sit in easily, and avoid very stiff or tight denim.

What shoes are best for a school trip?

Comfortable, supportive sneakers are the best choice because you'll likely walk a lot. Pick a pair you've already worn in, with good grip for outdoor paths and buses.
