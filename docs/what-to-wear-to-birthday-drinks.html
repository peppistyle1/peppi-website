What to wear to birthday drinks

For birthday drinks in the evening, smart casual is the safest choice: polished enough to feel celebratory, relaxed enough to move around a bar or restaurant. Aim for one good piece, such as a blazer or a striking top, paired with comfortable, well-fitting bottoms and shoes you can stand in for a few hours.

Tips

  • Treat smart casual as your baseline: one dressed-up element plus one relaxed element keeps you from looking overdressed.
  • Evening temperatures often drop after dark, so bring a layer like a blazer, cardigan or light jacket you can take on and off.
  • Choose shoes you can stand in for hours; sneakers and block heels both work for drinks, while very high heels are harder to keep up with.
  • Pick one accent color, such as blush or red, and keep the rest of the outfit in neutrals like black, white or brown for a clean look.
  • Add one statement detail, like a gold necklace, a bold earring or colored tights, rather than layering several competing accessories.

What to avoid

  • Avoid wearing a full suit or cocktail dress unless the invitation specifically asks for dressy, since it can feel out of place at a casual gathering.
  • Avoid heavily scented perfume or cologne, which can be overwhelming in a small, crowded space.
  • Avoid stiff, new shoes you have not walked in; blisters can end a fun night early.

Questions

What is the dress code for birthday drinks?

Birthday drinks are usually smart casual, meaning you dress nicer than everyday clothes but not formally. Unless the invite says otherwise, a blazer or nice top with clean trousers or jeans is a safe middle ground.

Should I dress up more for a birthday drinks party?

A little more than usual is a good idea, since it is a celebration. Adding one polished piece, like a blazer or a bold top, shows you made an effort without making the group feel underdressed or overdressed.

Can I wear sneakers to birthday drinks?

Yes, clean white or minimal sneakers work well for smart-casual drinks, especially paired with trousers or flared jeans. For a dressier feel, choose a sleek leather pair and avoid worn-out or athletic styles.
