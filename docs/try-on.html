How it works

From your photo to your look in under a minute.

  1. Add your photos

    One full-body photo works. A second one from the side makes the result and your measurements more accurate.

  2. Peppi checks them

    Your whole body, a clear face, good light. If something's off, Peppi tells you exactly what, so you can retake it.

  3. Tap Try it on

    On any saved look, or right on an outfit once you've saved it. Try on up to three at once and keep browsing: Peppi tells you when each one is ready.

  4. Keep it, or try again

    Keep the ones you like for 14 days. Swipe between them, turn each one around in 360°, and double-tap to zoom in on the details.

Your measurements

Know your size before you shop.

From your photos, Peppi estimates seven body measurements and your likely top and bottom sizes. Add your height and the estimates get more accurate; add a side-view photo and they can reach High accuracy.

Sizes vary between brands, so compare with each shop's size chart.

360° view

Walk around your outfit.

On any try-on, tap See it in 360°. Peppi's AI draws you from the left, the back and the right, using your side-view photo for your real profile. It's made in the background, so you can keep browsing, and it's included with your try-on.

Once it's ready, a ruler under the picture turns you around, right where the try-on is. Double-tap to zoom in, and keep turning.

It's made once and kept with the try-on. The sides and back are drawn from the try-on, so parts it doesn't show may differ.

How to take the best photo

A good photo makes a good try-on.

  • Show your face clearly. No sunglasses, mask or hand in front of it.
  • Skip the mirror selfie. The phone hides your face. Ask someone, or use a timer.
  • No hat, cap or beanie. If you always wear one, keep it: the try-on keeps it on rather than invent hair.
  • Head to feet, standing straight. Arms a little away from your body, in fitted everyday clothes.
  • Bright, even light. A plain background helps too.
  • Camera at chest height, 2 to 3 meters away. For photo 2, turn sideways.

Your photos stay yours

  • Nothing is uploaded until you agree, once, that these are photos of you and that you're 18 or older.
  • Photos are stored privately and only shown to you. Google's Gemini AI uses them to measure you and make each try-on.
  • Delete or replace them at any time. Leaving Couture or deleting your account deletes them and every try-on.
  • Try-ons you don't keep are deleted within 24 hours. Kept ones are deleted after 14 days.
  • If you don't use virtual try-on for 60 days, your photos are deleted automatically.

Good to know

  • Virtual try-on is for Couture members aged 18 and over, to keep young people's photos safe. Younger members still get Couture's outfits and Style DNA.
  • 20 try-ons and 10 photo uploads a month. 360° views are included, at no extra cost.
  • Peppi checks every result is still you. If it can't get the look right, you're told and no try-on is used.
  • Results are created with AI. They're an approximation and may not always look as expected.
