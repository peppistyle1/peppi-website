What to wear to a family lunch

For a family lunch, aim for casual chic: put together and comfortable, a step above what you'd wear to run errands. A neat top, trousers or a skirt, and shoes you can sit in all afternoon is a safe choice that suits most families. Start with the weather and the time of day, then choose how polished you want to look.

Tips

  • Match the dress code to the host: if it's a home lunch, go one step more polished than you'd wear to a casual brunch with friends.
  • At around 14°C, dress in layers: a cardigan, jacket or light knit you can take off once you're inside or in the sun.
  • Choose shoes you can walk and sit in comfortably; leather loafers, low heels or clean sneakers all work, depending on the look.
  • Stick to two or three colors, such as neutrals with one accent shade, so the outfit looks put together.
  • Keep details simple: a watch, small earrings or a belt are enough, and avoid anything that will get in the way at the table.

What to avoid

  • Heavy or overly formal outfits like a suit or cocktail dress, which can feel out of place at a relaxed family meal.
  • Very tight or revealing clothes, since you'll be sitting, bending and reaching for dishes all afternoon.
  • Brand-new shoes or outfits you haven't worn before, in case they rub or feel uncomfortable by dessert.

Questions

What is the best outfit for a family lunch?

A smart-casual outfit is usually safest: neat trousers or a skirt with a clean top and a layer you can remove. The Classic look, a cardigan over a white shirt with khaki trousers, is a reliable choice for most families.

Is jeans okay for a family lunch?

Yes, if they're dark, well-fitted and paired with a nicer top and shoes. Avoid distressed or faded pairs unless the lunch is very casual or outdoors. For a more polished look, trousers or a midi skirt work better.

What should a man wear to a family lunch?

Chinos or dark trousers with a button-down or fine knit, and loafers or clean leather shoes, works well. Add a light jacket or cardigan for cooler weather, and skip a tie unless the family is especially formal.
