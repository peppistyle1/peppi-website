What to wear for a day in the city

For a day in the city, aim for casual chic: polished enough for a café or a gallery, comfortable enough to walk for hours. Choose one structured layer, shoes you can walk in, and a palette of two or three colors. Peppi checks the weather where you'll be and builds outfits around it, so you can start from a look that already works.

Tips

  • Dress for the dress code: casual chic means nice fabrics and clean fit, not formal wear or gym clothes.
  • At 9°C in the morning, layer. A coat or overshirt you can open or take off covers the temperature swing before noon.
  • Wear shoes you've already walked in. City days often mean 10,000 steps, so break in boots or trainers before the day.
  • Limit your palette to two or three colors, or one bold color plus neutrals, so the outfit looks deliberate.
  • Keep the details tidy: a belt that matches your shoes, a bag that fits your daily essentials, and no items you'll need to adjust all day.

What to avoid

  • Heels or stiff leather soles you can't walk comfortably in for hours.
  • A single thin layer when the morning is cold; you'll be uncomfortable by midday or caught out by the wind.
  • Too many competing prints or logos, which looks busy rather than styled.

Questions

What should I wear for a day in the city in cold weather?

Layer a structured outer piece, like a trench or wool coat, over a knit or shirt, and wear closed shoes with good grip. Choose items you can open or remove as the day warms up.

Is jeans okay for a city day out?

Yes, as long as they're dark, well-fitted, and paired with a polished top or outer layer. Add shoes that look neat, such as clean trainers or loafers, to keep the look casual chic.

What shoes are best for walking around the city all day?

Choose comfortable shoes with cushioning and a flexible sole, such as clean trainers, loafers with a rubber sole, or boots you've already broken in. Avoid anything you haven't walked in before.
