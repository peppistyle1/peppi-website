Beach vacation outfits

For a beach vacation, aim for light, breathable clothes that are easy to throw on over swimwear and stay comfortable in the heat. Keep the look relaxed but put together, with one or two pieces that make it feel like a day out rather than the gym. When in doubt, choose loose fabrics, neutral or airy colors, and shoes you can walk sand in.

Classic, bold or relaxed.

Tips

  • Match the dress code to the spot: a beach town dinner calls for something a step up from a day at the sand.
  • Bring a light layer like an overshirt or linen wrap for air-conditioned restaurants and breezy evenings.
  • Choose sandals or canvas sneakers you can rinse off; leather and suede don't handle salt water well.
  • Stick to a palette of whites, creams, pastels or one bold print, and keep the rest neutral so pieces mix easily across days.
  • Protect yourself with a hat, sunglasses and a bag that holds sunscreen, water and a towel without weighing you down.

What to avoid

  • Heavy, stiff fabrics like thick denim or heavy knits, which trap heat and dry slowly after a swim.
  • Shoes with thin soles or delicate straps that sink into sand or slip on wet stone.
  • Too many dark colors in direct sun, which absorb heat and can feel stifling by mid-afternoon.

Questions

What should I wear to the beach on vacation?

Loose cotton, linen or light synthetic shorts or dresses, a breathable top, and sandals or sneakers you can get sandy. Add a hat and sunglasses and a cover-up for walking away from the water.

What do I wear to dinner after a beach day?

Swap the swim cover-up for a clean linen shirt, a midi or mini dress, or chinos with a tee. Keep the same easy shoes or switch to flat leather sandals if the restaurant is nicer.

What should men wear on a beach vacation?

Lightweight shorts or linen pants, a short-sleeve shirt or tee, and sandals or canvas sneakers. A bucket hat or cap and sunglasses finish the look, and a lightweight overshirt covers evenings.
