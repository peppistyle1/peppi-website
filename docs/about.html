About Peppi

Look great. Feel great.

Peppi is a personal stylist in your hand. It helps you decide what to wear for any occasion, weather or mood, and inspires you to find the look in your own wardrobe.

Why Peppi

Getting dressed shouldn't be the hard part of your day.

Putting together a shirt, trousers, shoes and a jacket that work for the occasion, the weather and the mood is harder than it looks. Peppi does the thinking: the right formality, layers for the temperature, colors that go together, and always a complete outfit.

It shows you three different directions every time, each with a real photo, so you can find a look you love and then make it yours with what you already own.

  • Built for every age from 13 up, with outfits that suit your age
  • Body shape is optional, and only ever used to guide fit, never mentioned
  • Styles inspired by fashion icons are shown through everyday people
  • Your photos and data are kept only as long as Peppi needs them
Where Peppi is going

From inspiration to your own wardrobe.

  1. Ready

    Outfits for any occasion

    Three complete outfits with real photos, matched to the occasion, weather and formality.

  2. Ready

    Style DNA and virtual try-on

    Peppi learns your style from your saved looks, and shows looks on you.

  3. Next

    Your own wardrobe

    Add the clothes you own, and Peppi will build outfits from them first.

  4. Later

    Shops

    Connect with stores, so what you buy goes straight into your wardrobe.
