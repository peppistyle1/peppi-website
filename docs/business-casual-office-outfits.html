Business casual outfits for the office

Business casual means polished but not formal: pieces that look intentional, fit well, and let you move comfortably through a workday. The safest approach is to choose tailored basics in neutral colors and add one considered detail, so you look put together whether the office is relaxed or more traditional.

Tips

  • Read the dress code first: if your office is casual, the Relaxed look is often enough; if clients visit often, lean toward the Classic.
  • At 12°C in the morning, layer rather than bulk up. A knit, a blazer, or a light coat you can take off indoors works better than one heavy piece.
  • Choose shoes you can walk in all day. Oxfords, loafers, and low heels look professional without making you uncomfortable by afternoon.
  • Keep your palette to two or three colors. Neutrals like black, cream, tan, and navy are easy to mix and make the outfit look planned.
  • Pay attention to fit at the shoulders and hem, and choose a simple watch or small jewelry as the one detail that finishes the look.

What to avoid

  • Athletic wear, distressed denim, and graphic tees, which read as too casual for most offices.
  • Wrinkled or ill-fitting clothes; a well-pressed basic beats an expensive piece that does not fit.
  • Too many competing patterns or loud logos, which pull attention away from your work.

Questions

What is business casual for the office?

Business casual sits between formal business attire and weekend clothes. Think tailored trousers or skirts, knits, collared shirts, blazers, and closed-toe shoes, in clean, neutral colors that fit well.

Can men wear business casual without a suit jacket?

Yes. A collared shirt or fine knit with tailored chinos or trousers and leather shoes or loafers is standard business casual. Add a blazer when you want a more formal presence or meet clients.

What should I wear to the office when it is cold?

Layer lightweight pieces such as a fine knit, a shirt under a sweater or blazer, and a coat you can remove at your desk. Keep the layers in the same color family so the outfit still looks cohesive.
