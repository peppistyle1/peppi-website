What to wear to a garden party

For a garden party in the afternoon, aim for smart casual: polished enough to look intentional, light enough to stay comfortable in the sun. Breathable fabrics, a little color and sun protection will serve you better than anything formal or heavy.

Classic, bold or relaxed.

Tips

  • Read the invitation: a 'garden party' usually means smart casual, but a note like 'black tie optional' or 'cocktail attire' changes everything.
  • Choose breathable fabrics such as linen, cotton, or lightweight blends, especially above about 20°C.
  • Bring a layer for when the sun drops. A light cardigan, jacket, or wrap is easy to carry and useful in the evening.
  • Wear shoes you can walk in on grass. Block heels, wedges, flats, or loafers work better than stilettos, which sink into lawns.
  • Pick one statement piece, such as a bold color, a hat, or a patterned fabric, and keep the rest of the outfit simpler.

What to avoid

  • Heavy, dark fabrics like wool or thick denim, which trap heat in the afternoon sun.
  • Very long or very dragging hems and stiletto heels, which catch on grass and pavers.
  • Wearing all black or overly formal evening wear unless the invitation specifically asks for it.

Questions

What should I wear to a garden party in the summer?

Choose light, breathable fabrics like cotton, linen, or a light floral print in a comfortable cut. Add sun protection such as a hat or sunglasses, and keep your shoes comfortable for walking on grass.

What do men wear to a garden party?

A lightweight suit or a linen shirt with chinos works well, with loafers or suede shoes instead of dress shoes. Avoid a full heavy suit unless the invitation calls for a formal dress code. A panama or straw hat is a classic finishing touch.

Are hats appropriate at a garden party?

Yes, a sun hat or a straw or panama hat is one of the most fitting accessories for a garden party. Choose one that suits the outfit, and take it off indoors or when the host asks.
